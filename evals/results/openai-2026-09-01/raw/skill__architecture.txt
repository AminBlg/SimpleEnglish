sqlpipe has three main parts. A reader process streams rows from Postgres in batches. The reader collects several rows before sending them. A converter turns each batch into a Parquet row group. The converter formats the data for efficient storage and queries. An uploader writes objects to Amazon S3 as multipart uploads. Multipart uploads split large files into smaller parts. The uploader retries parts that fail to upload. This process makes the data transfer reliable. Together, these parts move data from Postgres to S3 in a structured and fault-tolerant way. This design helps sqlpipe handle large data efficiently while guarding against network or service interruptions.
