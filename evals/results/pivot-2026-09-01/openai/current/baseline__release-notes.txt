sqlpipe v2.0 is here with significant improvements and an important breaking change. The `/v2/users` endpoint’s `name` field has been split into separate `first_name` and `last_name` fields to enhance data clarity and flexibility—please update your integrations accordingly. We've introduced a new `--incremental` flag, enabling efficient incremental data processing for faster, more resource-friendly pipelines. Parquet write performance has been optimized, resulting in up to 40% faster data exports, accelerating your workflows and reducing latency. This release focuses on improving data handling and operational efficiency while requiring a quick adjustment for user-related APIs. Upgrade to sqlpipe v2.0 to take advantage of these enhancements and ensure compatibility with the updated user schema.
