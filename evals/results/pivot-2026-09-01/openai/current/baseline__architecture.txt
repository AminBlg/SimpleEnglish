SQLPipe is a data pipeline designed to efficiently transfer and convert data from a Postgres database to S3 storage in Parquet format. The architecture consists of three primary components. First, the reader process streams rows from Postgres in manageable batches, ensuring optimized memory usage and stable throughput. Next, the converter transforms each batch of rows into Parquet row groups, leveraging Parquet’s columnar storage benefits for efficient querying and compression. Finally, the uploader handles the transmission of these Parquet row groups as multipart objects to Amazon S3. It incorporates robust retry mechanisms to ensure data durability and successful uploads, even in the face of transient network or service disruptions. This modular architecture promotes scalability, fault tolerance, and efficient handling of large-scale data transfers.
