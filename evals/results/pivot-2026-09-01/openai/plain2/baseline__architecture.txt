SQLPipe is designed to efficiently export data from Postgres to S3 in Parquet format. It begins with a reader process that streams rows from a Postgres database in configurable batch sizes. These batches are passed to a converter component that transforms the row data into Parquet row groups, optimizing for columnar storage and compression. Once converted, the uploader component handles writing the data to S3 as multipart objects, enabling large files to be uploaded reliably and efficiently. The uploader incorporates retry logic to handle transient network or service errors, ensuring durability and fault tolerance in the upload process. This modular architecture, with clear separation between reading, conversion, and uploading, supports scalability and adaptability for various data volumes and workloads.
