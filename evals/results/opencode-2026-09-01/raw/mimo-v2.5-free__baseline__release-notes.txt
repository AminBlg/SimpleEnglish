## sqlpipe v2.0

**Breaking change:** The `name` field on `/v2/users` has been removed. Use `first_name` and `last_name` instead. This change improves consistency with downstream systems and eliminates ambiguous parsing for multi-byte names.

**New: `--incremental` flag.** Run sqlpipe with `--incremental` to process only new or changed records since the last run. This reduces redundant reads on large tables and simplifies scheduled sync jobs.

**Performance:** Parquet write speed is now 40% faster, cutting end-to-end export time for large datasets significantly.

Upgrade early to benefit from the incremental mode and faster writes. Refer to the migration guide for details on the `/v2/users` schema change.
