## Troubleshooting

### Connection Timeouts to PostgreSQL

If sqlpipe times out connecting to Postgres, verify the host, port, and database name in your configuration. Ensure the PostgreSQL server is running and accepting connections. Check that `pg_hba.conf` allows connections from your client IP. If you're behind a VPN or firewall, confirm that port 5432 (or your custom port) is open. Test connectivity with `psql` before running sqlpipe. For remote databases, verify DNS resolution and check for network latency. If using SSL, ensure certificate paths are correct and not expired.

### S3 Permission Errors

S3 permission errors typically indicate the IAM credentials lack required permissions. The service account needs `s3:PutObject`, `s3:GetObject`, and `s3:ListBucket` on the target bucket. Verify your AWS credentials are configured correctly via environment variables, `~/.aws/credentials`, or IAM roles. Check that the bucket policy allows uploads from your account. If using a VPC endpoint, ensure the endpoint policy grants access. For server-side encryption, confirm the KMS key policy permits the service account to use it.
