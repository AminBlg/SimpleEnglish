Sqlpipe operates as a three‑stage pipeline for bulk exporting Postgres data to S3 as Parquet. A reader process connects to Postgres, fetches rows in configurable batches and passes each batch downstream. A converter transforms the batch into a Parquet row group, applying columnar encoding, compression, and schema evolution as needed. Finally, an uploader streams the Parquet data as a multipart S3 object, handling network errors with exponential back‑off retries and ensuring each part reaches the destination before committing the final object. The pipeline can be horizontally scaled by adding parallel reader instances, while the converter and uploader remain stateless, allowing flexible deployment on containers or serverless functions. Each stage emits metrics to Prometheus, enabling observability of throughput, latency, and error rates across the overall pipeline. Alerts trigger when batch latency exceeds thresholds systematically.
