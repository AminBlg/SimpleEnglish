Yes, 2 million messages of consumer lag is serious and warrants immediate attention. It means your consumers are falling behind producers — messages are piling up in the topic partitions faster than your application is reading and processing them. For users, the practical effect depends on what those consumers drive: if they feed real-time features (notifications, order status, search indexing, analytics), users will see those features become stale or delayed by however long it takes to drain that backlog. If the lag keeps growing rather than stabilizing, it will eventually cause out-of-memory pressure on brokers or trigger log retention cutoffs, which means your consumers could lose messages entirely before they process them.

The most useful next questions are: is the lag growing, stable, or draining? And is it spread across all partitions or concentrated in a few? A stable lag at 2M can be acceptable for a batch pipeline with generous SLAs, but a climbing lag in a real-time pipeline is a genuine incident.
