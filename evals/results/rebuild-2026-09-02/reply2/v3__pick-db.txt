Before sharding, add indexes on your query predicates and check `EXPLAIN (ANALYZE, BUFFERS)` on your slowest queries. Sharding is operationally expensive and almost never the right first move at 40M rows, which is modest by Postgres standards. If indexes do not help, look at partitioning (by date or tenant ID) — it gives query pruning without the cross-shard join complexity. Only reach for sharding if you have exhausted indexing, partitioning, read replicas for read scaling, and connection pooling via PgBouncer.
