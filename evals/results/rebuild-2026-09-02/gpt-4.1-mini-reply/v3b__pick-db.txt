First, analyze the query patterns and identify which columns appear in filters or joins. Then, check if indexing those columns improves read speed. If indexes help but reads remain slow, consider partitioning the table by a key that splits data logically and evenly. Only after that, evaluate sharding, which divides data across multiple servers and adds complexity. Sharding needs careful planning and operational support, so gather metrics and prove simpler solutions do not solve your issue first.
