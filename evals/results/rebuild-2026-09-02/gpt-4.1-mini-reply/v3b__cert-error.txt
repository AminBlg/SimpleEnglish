Curl reports that it cannot verify the SSL certificate of your internal API because it cannot find the certificate authority (CA) that issued the server certificate. The client needs the CA bundle of trusted root and intermediate certificates to trust the server. To fix this, you must obtain the CA certificate that issued your server’s certificate and add it to curl’s trusted CA bundle or specify it with the --cacert option. Alternatively, you can disable the certificate check using --insecure, but this weakens security. Make sure that the server’s certificate chain is complete and correctly configured.
