The users see stale prices because the Redis cache holds old data until it expires or refreshes. You update the database, but Redis still serves cached prices for up to 10 minutes by design. To fix this, you must clear or update the Redis cache immediately after updating the database. You can do this by deleting the relevant Redis keys or writing new prices to Redis at the same time. This method keeps the cache and database synchronized and avoids stale data shown to users.
