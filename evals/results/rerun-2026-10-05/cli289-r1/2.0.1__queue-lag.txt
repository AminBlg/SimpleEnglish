Yes, 2 million messages of consumer lag is serious. It means your consumer group is falling behind the producers at a rate faster than it can process, and the gap is growing. Users will see delayed data — anything that depends on that topic (notifications, analytics, order status, search indexes) reflects events from however far back that lag represents in wall-clock time, which depends entirely on your throughput rate. If your producers push 10,000 messages per second, 2 million means users see data that is roughly 3 minutes stale and getting worse. The risk is not just latency: if lag keeps growing and your Kafka retention window expires before consumers catch up, those messages are permanently lost.
