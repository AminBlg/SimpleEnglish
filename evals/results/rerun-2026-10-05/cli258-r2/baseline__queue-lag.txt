**Yes, that's bad.** Here's what it means:

## What consumer lag means

Lag = messages produced minus messages consumed. At 2M lag, your consumers are 2 million messages *behind* what producers have written. They're falling further behind faster than they can catch up.

## Impact on users

Depends entirely on your **throughput rate**:

| Throughput | 2M lag = |
|---|---|
| 10k msg/s | ~3 min delay |
| 100k msg/s | ~20 sec delay |
| 1k msg/s | ~33 min delay |

The lag number alone doesn't tell you severity — the **rate of change** does. If lag is climbing, that's the crisis. If it plateaued, consumers are keeping pace but haven't caught up yet.

**User-facing symptoms typically include:**
- Delayed notifications, emails, or webhooks
- Stale read-your-writes (user takes an action, doesn't see it reflected)
- Order processing, payments, or analytics falling behind real-time
- Downstream services (search indexes, caches, recommendation engines) showing old data

## Likely causes

1. **Consumer too slow** — processing logic is the bottleneck (DB calls, external API, CPU-heavy work)
2. **Under-partitioned topic** — you can't add more consumer instances than you have partitions
3. **Consumer crash/rebalance loop** — group is constantly rebalancing, no real processing happening
4. **Producer spike** — sudden burst that outpaced normal consumer capacity
5. **Bad message** — one poison pill causing repeated failures and retries

## What to check first

```bash
# Lag per partition — is it one partition or spread across all?
kafka-consumer-groups.sh --bootstrap-server <host> --describe --group <group>

# Is lag growing or stable?
# Watch the LAG column over 30s intervals
```

If lag is concentrated on **one partition**, you likely have a poison pill or a single slow consumer. If it's **spread evenly**, you're under-provisioned for the current load.

The immediate question: **is it growing right now?** If yes, you need more consumer parallelism or to find and skip/DLQ the blocking message. If it peaked and stabilized, you're catching up and can assess calmly.
