The staleness comes from Redis caching the price at read time with a TTL of up to 10 minutes. When you write a new price to the database, the old value stays in Redis until that TTL expires, so reads keep returning the cached price.

The fix is to invalidate the Redis key at write time. When you update a price in the database, delete the corresponding Redis key in the same operation. The next read will miss the cache, fetch the fresh price from the database, and repopulate Redis.

If you need stricter consistency, lower the TTL, but cache invalidation on write is the right primary fix.
