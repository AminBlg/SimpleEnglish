sqlpipe v2.0.0

**Breaking Changes**
- `/v2/users` endpoint: `name` field replaced with `first_name` and `last_name`. Update your consumers.

**New Features**
- `--incremental` flag enables incremental syncs for supported sources. Reads only changed rows since last run, reducing load and latency.

**Performance**
- Parquet writes are ~40% faster via columnar batch flushing and reduced allocation overhead. Larger datasets see proportionally greater gains.

**Migration**
```bash
# Before
curl /v2/users | jq '.[] | .name'

# After
curl /v2/users | jq '.[] | "\(.first_name) \(.last_name)"'
```
