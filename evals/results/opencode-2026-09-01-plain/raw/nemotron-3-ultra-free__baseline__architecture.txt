sqlpipe streams Postgres data to S3 as Parquet through three decoupled processes connected by bounded queues. The reader executes a configurable SELECT, fetches rows in batches using server-side cursors, and pushes typed record batches downstream. The converter receives batches, validates schemas, applies type coercion, and assembles Parquet row groups with dictionary encoding and statistics for predicate pushdown. The uploader consumes row groups, buffers them to disk, and writes multipart S3 objects with exponential-backoff retries and checksum verification. Each stage runs independently: the reader pauses when the converter queue fills, the converter pauses when the uploader queue fills, providing natural backpressure. Failed uploads retry per-part without re-reading Postgres. A coordinator manages lifecycle, handles graceful shutdown on SIGTERM, and emits progress metrics. Configuration via CLI or file controls batch size, row group target size, concurrency, and S3 multipart thresholds.
